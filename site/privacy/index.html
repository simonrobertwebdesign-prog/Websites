Privacy notice

Last updated 18 November 2026

Who I am

Simon Robert, trading as Simon Robert Web Design, Oxfordshire. You can contact me about anything on this page at simonrobert.webdesign@gmail.com.

What I collect

  • If you send an enquiry: your name, business name, email address, current website and anything you write in your message.
  • If I contact your business: your business name and the business contact details you publish (for example on your own website or on Which? Trusted Traders), plus notes on what I found when I looked at your current website.
  • For a free preview: details your business has published, such as services, areas covered and public customer reviews.

Why I use it

To reply to enquiries, and to offer website services to businesses that could benefit from them. My lawful basis is legitimate interests. I only send unrequested emails to limited companies, every email says who I am, and you can opt out at any time.

Free previews

A preview is made only for the business it names. It is clearly marked as a preview, it is hidden from search engines, and it is not linked from anywhere else on this site. I take a preview down straight away if you ask, and within 90 days if you’re not interested.

Who else handles it

Emails are handled by Google (Gmail). This website, its forms and the previews are hosted by Netlify. I keep my own private records of who I have contacted. I never sell or share your details for anyone else’s marketing.

How long I keep it

Records of enquiries and outreach are kept for up to 12 months after we were last in touch. If you become a customer, I keep what’s needed for the work and for my accounts (accounting records are kept for 6 years). If you ask me not to contact you again, I keep just your business name and email on a do-not-contact list so that I never email you again.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information I hold about you, ask me to correct or delete it, or object to me using it. Email me and I’ll deal with it within one month. To stop emails, just reply “stop” to any email from me.

If you’re unhappy with how I’ve handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
