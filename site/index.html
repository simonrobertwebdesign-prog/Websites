Questions

The small print.

Do I need my own web address?+

Yes. You buy your own web address (for example yourbusiness.co.uk), usually about £10 a year, so it’s always yours. I’ll show you how and connect it for you.

Who owns the website?+

You do, once it’s paid for in full.

Where do the photos come from?+

Your own photos wherever possible. For general background images I use licensed free stock photos, and I never pass them off as your work.

Will it get me to the top of Google?+

No one can honestly promise that. I set up the basics properly, like page titles and descriptions, and a fast site that works well on phones.

What if I don’t like the preview?+

Then you don’t pay anything, and I won’t keep emailing you.

How quickly do you reply?+

The same working day.
